# Changelog

Notable changes, newest first. Dates are when the work was merged or finished.

## Unreleased
- Renamed the feature sets: V1 (15 features) is now `feature_list_15` and V2 (38) is `feature_list_38`, in `src/features/build_features.py` (`FEATURE_LIST_15`, `FEATURE_LIST_38`), `config/config.yaml`, `config/holdout.yaml`, `src/config.py` and the tests. The old names clashed with the forecast log's model version `v1` and the `v1`/`v2` school calendars. The columns and their order are unchanged (checked against the lists before the rename). The experiment log (Series 19-22), the Series 20 scripts and their stored results keep the old labels, which is what those results were written with.
- Added an optional warm look to the chat assistant's page (`src/assistant/theme.py`, `app.py`): a small "Switch page style" button turns on a hospitality-style theme (deep navy, saffron, terracotta, teal and cream; serif headings; a navy header; saffron guest bubbles and navy assistant bubbles; the privacy notice as a full-width band) and remembers the choice in the visitor's browser. The page still opens in Gradio's normal look, and every warm rule is scoped under a `warm` class on the page body, so with the look off nothing changes. It looks the same on a dark system and down to a phone. Fonts load from Google Fonts, now stated in the privacy notice and the README. Behaviour, tools and the wording of answers are unchanged. New tests in `tests/test_assistant_theme.py` guard the privacy notice, that no warm rule leaks outside its scope, and the absence of any third-party branding.
- Series 34 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track C): recency weighting, with half-lives of 45, 90, 180, 365 and 730 days passed to CatBoost as sample weights. The final model gets steadily worse as the weighting sharpens (45 days: +£158; 90 days: +£31; 730 days, barely a weighting: +£1.5), because the effective sample size falls from about 635 rows to as few as 126; the least bad fails its bar on fresh seeds (Friday +£20, significantly worse). Track C step two is not run. New script `run_series34_recency_weights.py` and tests.
- Series 33 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`: the first-week correction forward-chained on the leave-one-break-out forecasts of all six Easter and summer breaks, pooled mean against per-type against pooled median. Over the four breaks every variant can correct, the median improves the first-week MAE by £125.5 a day (mean £86.6, per type £47.1); splitting by kind of break costs data for no gain, and every variant over-corrects summer 2026 because the model's own bias on first weeks is shrinking as more breaks enter its training. The pooled-median rule is pre-registered for the Easter and summer 2027 breaks. New script `run_series33_first_week_by_type.py` and tests.
- Series 32 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track B): a correction for the first week of the Easter and summer breaks. The model under-forecasts that week in all six breaks in the data, 2024 included (forecasts from a model trained without a window around each break), by 2% to 12%. A correction learned from other breaks improves five of the six breaks (additive, first week only: -£105 to -£178 on the first-week days, interval below zero) but the pre-registered forward-chained test has only three scorable breaks and fails its bar, so it is parked and the exact rule is pre-registered for the Easter and summer 2027 breaks. Recorded a flaw in the step 1 selection rule (it compared MAE across scopes with different days). New script `run_series32_first_week_correction.py` and tests.
- Re-validated the Track A package (Series 23-27) end to end on the verified school calendar, including every selection step, and logged it as Series 31. It holds: £864.63 against the final model's £908.21 (-4.8%) on seeds 0-24 and £865.23 against £908.92 on fresh seeds (-4.8%), the same as on the original calendar to within £1; the same correction settings and the same Saturday and Sunday options win, only Friday's option changed (`A_summary` to `A_all`), which fits Friday being noise. Added `src/experiments/run_package_validation.py` (one table for any two result sets) and a `SERIES_RESULTS_SUFFIX` environment variable that sends a whole re-run to its own folders. The Series 23 safety check for the original £909.57 now applies only to the original results set. New tests in `tests/test_package_validation.py`.
- Series 30 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track B step two): features that describe where a day sits in a school break (days since the break started, days until it ends, days until the next one, and first-week, last-week-of-term and last-week-of-break flags), four variants on both targets. None helps; every variant is worse than the baseline on the transition weeks (+£9.5 to +£24.1) and the best fails the bar on fresh seeds (+£11.7, interval -£8.9 to +£30.7). The excess error in the first week of a break comes from a handful of event-like days (the start of the summer holidays, Boxing Day): without the eight worst first-week days the MAE is £886. Nothing is added to the model. New script `run_series30_transition_features.py` and tests.
- Verified the school calendar against independent sources and corrected it (`docs/SCHOOL_CALENDAR.md`, `SCHOOL_BREAKS` in `src/features/build_features.py`). The original table started at Christmas 2024 (2024's Easter and summer holidays were term time), used weekday-only spans and had several wrong dates, for example Easter 2026 a week out and summer 2025 starting four days late (so the busiest day in the data, Saturday 28 June 2025, was labelled term time). Breaks now run from the first pupil-free day to the day before pupils return; Christmas, Easter and summer 2024 to 2027 are covered, and so are the October week and February mid-term (new break types `october` and `february`, flagged by the pipeline, the API and the assistant; the API now says "October week" or "February mid-term break"). The council's page was checked for 2026-27 from screenshots; earlier years rest on at least two independent sources each. The original is kept as `SCHOOL_BREAKS_V1` to reproduce Series 1-28. Series 29 shows the corrected calendar changes the final model's MAE by about nothing (£909.57 to £908.21 with all five break types), so the transition weeks stay the hard days. New tests in `tests/test_school_calendar.py` and `tests/test_series29.py`.
- Series 28 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track A item 5): a diagnostic of whether the manager's forecast knows more than the weekend package. It does not on Saturday or Sunday (correlation of the package's miss with the manager's disagreement -0.03 and -0.08; blending the manager in does not help), only weakly Monday to Friday. Writes lists of the 20 weekend days and 20 days overall where the manager beat the package most, with a blank "What happened?" column, and a printable page. New script `run_series28_manager_diagnostic.py` and tests.
- Series 27 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track A item 4 follow-up): each of Friday, Saturday and Sunday chooses its own feature option on the first five test blocks and is judged on the last five. Chosen: Friday the mean and highest of the last 4 Fridays, Saturday sales 2 and 3 weeks ago, Sunday the weekend interactions. On the judging blocks and fresh seeds Friday to Sunday improves by £51 (95% interval -£88 to -£20; Saturday -£101, Friday -£29, Sunday -£24), so the pre-registered bar passes; with the Series 24 correction the scored-block MAE is £862.45 against the final model's £905.63. Near-term forecasts only for Friday and Saturday; not promoted. New script `run_series27_weekend_per_weekday.py` and tests.
- Series 26 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track A item 4): weekend-specific features, same-weekday memory (sales 2 and 3 weeks ago, the average and highest of the last 4 same weekdays) and weekend interactions, on both targets (10 runs of 25 seeds). Sales 2 and 3 weeks ago lowers Friday to Sunday by £36 on fresh seeds (Saturday £97) but worsens Monday to Thursday by £13, so the pre-registered bar fails; used for Friday to Sunday only it gives £877.7 against the hybrid's £893.0 and the final model's £909.1 (a post-hoc hypothesis, near-term forecasts only). The interactions add nothing. New script `run_series26_weekend_features.py` and tests.
- Series 25 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track A item 3): training the model with the weekends in mind. Weighting Friday to Sunday rows 1.5, 2 or 3 times, and separate Friday-to-Sunday and Saturday-only models, each on the log and raw targets (10 runs of 25 seeds): every variant is worse than the current weekend setup on Friday to Sunday (+£7 to +£69), so nothing is added. Separate models lose because they see far fewer rows. New script `run_series25_weekend_training.py` and tests.
- Series 24 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (accuracy roadmap, Track A item 1): an adaptive Friday-to-Sunday correction on a hybrid that uses the raw-£ model on Saturdays and the log model elsewhere. Pre-registered 9-variant grid, confirmed on 25 fresh seeds: the hybrid is worth £15.8 and the correction a further £11.7 (95% interval -£24.4 to -£0.9), £27.5 (3.0%) against the final model on the same 540 days. The Friday correction is harmful in all 9 variants. Passes the pre-registered bar narrowly; not promoted, needs confirmation on new days. New script `run_series24_weekend_correction.py` and tests.
- Series 23 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (first step of the accuracy roadmap, Track A): does the log1p target hold back Saturdays and the busy days? Against the same CatBoost on raw pounds (25 seeds, final protocol), the log target costs about £113 of Saturday MAE (95% interval £24 to £210) and £240 to £316 of the £720 Saturday under-forecast, but is better on quiet days, so the overall figures are level (£909.57 against £916.75 and £913.12). The log target is kept. New script `run_series23_log_vs_raw_peaks.py` (resumable; reproduces £909.57 exactly).
- Series 19-22 added to `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`: column order (100 random orders plus a seed-luck control), the LSTM and Transformer on the 38 features (grid search, fresh-seed confirmation), a CatBoost depth-and-iterations grid (60 configurations), and the networks given the predicted day's own features. None improves on the final model. New scripts `run_series19_column_order.py`, `run_series20_neural_v2.py`, `run_series21_catboost_depth_iterations.py`, `run_series22_neural_target_day.py`, a shared resumable harness (`series19_21_common.py`) and a report builder (`report_series19_21.py`); the harness reproduces the final model's £909.57 exactly.
- `build_windows` / `prepare_datasets` in `src/models/neural_common.py` gained an opt-in `include_target_day` switch (off by default; earlier results unchanged).
- Added `src/experiments/run_error_autopsy.py`: an analysis of where the final model misses (the 20 biggest surges and lulls with calendar, holiday and weather context and a blank column for the venue manager, error by kind of day, whether the manager's forecast knew more, and a forward-chained test of weekday bias corrections). Findings: Friday to Sunday carry 55% of the error; Saturdays are usually under-forecast but the bias fades; the first week of a school break and the last week of term carry about 30-40% more error than their share of days.
- Activated `config/config.yaml`, which was previously unused: a strict, validated loader (`src/config.py`, Pydantic) now supplies the paths, split dates, weather thresholds and coordinates, XGBoost/SARIMAX/CatBoost settings, analysis defaults and each model's feature set. About 35 hard-coded path constants and a dozen duplicated settings across 25 files now read from it. The stale values in the old file were replaced with the ones the code actually used, so no result changes (the weather coordinates stay as they were).
- Feature sets: the original 15 features (V1) and all 38 (V2) are each defined once in `src/features/build_features.py` (previously copied into four and two files); `features.use` in the config picks the set per model.
- Removed `data/features/model_features.csv`: it was the V1 selection of `engineered_features.csv`, now `load_model_frame()`.
- Verified by running the whole pipeline before and after: all 585 reproducible output files are byte-identical (features, preprocessing, baselines, SARIMAX, XGBoost, the final model's backtest predictions and metrics, figures). `config/config.yaml` changes now retrain the model in CI and redeploy the API. New tests in `tests/test_config.py`.
- Added `kind=best` to `GET /errors/ranking` and a fourth ranking tool, `get_top_best_days`, so the assistant can list the days the forecast came closest (before, it could only list misses and said it had no tool for the best ones). A forecast that matches to the penny is reported as `exact`.
